# Keep building access behind the Road Edge Bands

Every Landmark Access Lane, Residential Driveway, and entrance-guidance spur will stop at the building-side edge of the roadside sidewalk; the sidewalk and both Road Edge Bands will remain visually continuous, with no access surface drawn across them or onto the main-road asphalt. This deliberately favors an unambiguous road hierarchy and artifact-free low-poly junctions over literal curb cuts or driveway aprons, which repeatedly read as raised obstructions, broken joints, or road intrusions at the world's scale and camera angle. All future Building Plots must preserve this boundary.
