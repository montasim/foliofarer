# Journey Experience

The Journey Experience presents Montasim's professional story through directly entered World Exploration and an always-available professional Journey Atlas. A Visitor can read the complete evidence without making a Physical Visit or relying on the Portfolio World.

## Language

**Journey Experience**:
The presentation of Montasim's professional chronology that enters World Exploration when the Portfolio World is available while keeping the complete Journey Atlas available throughout. When the Portfolio World is unavailable, the Journey Atlas is the entrance.
_Avoid_: Game, Portfolio World, 3D portfolio

**Standard Portfolio**:
The canonical presentation of Montasim's complete portfolio outside the Journey Experience and a shared source of professional evidence.
_Avoid_: Legacy portfolio, 2D game

**Portfolio Record**:
A canonical set of facts about one education, career, learning, project, or community item that may be presented by the Standard Portfolio, Journey Atlas, and World Exploration.
_Avoid_: Game copy, duplicated record

**Journey Atlas**:
The primary professional presentation within the Journey Experience, where every Portfolio Record can be read directly in chronological or Visitor-selected order without World Exploration or a Physical Visit.
_Avoid_: Map-first mode, fallback map, game menu, Journey Map

**Portfolio World**:
The spatial environment used during World Exploration. It provides place, travel and encounter locations but never owns professional evidence.
_Avoid_: Open world, level

**World Exploration**:
The optional playful mode in which a Visitor controls the Montasim Avatar through the Portfolio World. It adds spatial discovery and Journey Passport progress but never owns or gates professional evidence.
_Avoid_: Main portfolio, required game, Journey Atlas

**Physical Visit**:
Reaching a Landmark checkpoint during World Exploration. It may create a Journey Passport stamp and count toward optional Journey Completion, but never controls access to a Portfolio Record.
_Avoid_: Unlock, Atlas reading, content access

**Journey Experience Contract**:
The stable professional access, content, controls, navigation behavior, and interaction outcomes shared by the Journey Atlas and World Exploration. Portfolio World availability or Adaptive Render Tier may never remove professional evidence or make World Exploration mandatory.
_Avoid_: Pixel-identical rendering, frozen implementation, unchanged UI

**Adaptive Render Tier**:
World Exploration's current rendering-cost level, selected from device capability and sustained runtime performance. It may vary Environmental Scenery, shadows, and resolution but never changes Journey Atlas content or removes Landmarks, access routes, checkpoints, labels, guidance, or interaction cues.
_Avoid_: Device class, permanent low-quality mode, visual redesign

**Journey Entrance**:
The floating Standard Portfolio control that opens the full-screen Journey Experience directly into World Exploration when the Portfolio World is available, with immediate Journey Atlas and Journey Passport access and a clear return. When the Portfolio World is unavailable, it opens the Journey Atlas.
_Avoid_: Game modal, embedded canvas

**Visitor**:
A portfolio viewer who may read the Journey Atlas and optionally control the Montasim Avatar during World Exploration.
_Avoid_: User, player

**Montasim Avatar**:
The visitor-controlled representation of Montasim through whom the Portfolio World is explored and its stories are told in the first person.
_Avoid_: Player character, generic avatar

**Guided Journey**:
The chronological route through representative stories from About, Education, Career, Learning, Projects, and Community before Contact. It can be read in the Journey Atlas or physically followed during World Exploration without restricting movement.
_Avoid_: Mandatory tutorial, campaign

**District**:
A recognizable area of the Portfolio World that groups Landmarks belonging to one major portfolio subject.
_Avoid_: Page section, category menu

**Journey Completion**:
The optional World Exploration milestone reached by physically visiting the representative Guided Journey stop in every District and then reaching Contact. It is not completion of the professional portfolio and does not require opening every Archive item.
_Avoid_: Portfolio completion, one-hundred-percent completion

**Contact Pavilion**:
The concluding Journey Atlas destination and Landmark Encounter, where the primary invitation is to start a conversation with Montasim. Its name does not require a portfolio-representing environmental building; starting a conversation, ending the journey, and continuing World Exploration are separate choices.
_Avoid_: Finish screen, sales page

**Journey Summary**:
The closing view shown when a Visitor chooses to end World Exploration, reflecting their Physical Visits and providing paths to the Journey Atlas, Standard Portfolio, and Montasim's professional links.
_Avoid_: Game-over screen, score screen

**Landmark**:
A named spatial checkpoint associated with a portfolio subject and Portfolio Record. A neutral Environmental Scenery building may frame its arrival and carry an Arrival Sign, but neither the checkpoint nor that building owns or exclusively contains professional evidence.
_Avoid_: Environmental building, menu item, content gate

**Environmental Scenery**:
The non-semantic terrain, ocean, island, river, pond, bridge, road, ordinary house, vegetation, rock, cloud, and infrastructure that establish atmosphere and support travel. It never owns or contains a Portfolio Record, Discovery, skill, project, employer, institution, or professional claim. An Arrival Sign may name an adjacent Landmark solely for wayfinding without making its building professional evidence.
_Avoid_: Landmark, portfolio metaphor, professional evidence

**Arrival Sign**:
A small, façade-mounted, code-generated wayfinding nameplate on an arrival building. It may reproduce only the adjacent Landmark's canonical `shortTitle` so a Visitor can identify the place; it never carries evidence, a description, a logo, content state, an interaction, or an unlock. The DOM Journey Atlas, Place Card, Landmark Encounters and Journey Passport remain authoritative.
_Avoid_: Portfolio card, evidence panel, building interior, content state

**Monsoon Afternoon**:
The Portfolio World's bright, warm, illustrated Bangladeshi daylight environment, expressed through coastal water, dense vegetation, grounded roads, local building forms, soft atmospheric colour and restrained paper-like interfaces.
_Avoid_: Summer Afternoon clone, photorealistic simulation, generic green town

**Building Plot**:
The designated land occupied by an Environmental Scenery building, including its Roadside Setback, road-facing frontage, and an access connection appropriate to the building's use. A Building Plot never carries a Portfolio Record, and a building never sits directly on the main road or sidewalk.
_Avoid_: Arbitrary building position, roadside prop

**Landmark Access Lane**:
A short, vehicle-width paved branch from a set-back Arrival Forecourt to the main road. It meets the sidewalk, curb, drainage and asphalt through one compiled access junction rather than overlapping or stopping abruptly at them.
_Avoid_: Mini road, entrance walkway, footpath

**Residential Driveway**:
A narrow paved branch from a set-back roadside home to the main road. It crosses the sidewalk and Road Edge Bands only through one compiled access junction.
_Avoid_: Landmark access lane, footpath

**Road Edge Bands**:
The curb-and-drain boundary between main-road asphalt and its roadside sidewalk. It remains continuous except at an intentional compiled access or road junction where its interruption is part of the shared surface topology.
_Avoid_: Greenish lines, driveway ends, connector lines

**Roadside Setback**:
The clear frontage between a building and the outer edge of the main-road sidewalk. Larger public buildings use a deeper setback than roadside homes. Clearance is checked against the whole curved street—not only the nearest frontage—so no building, compound boundary, or Landmark checkpoint can occupy any road or sidewalk segment.
_Avoid_: Road offset, arbitrary gap

**Arrival Forecourt**:
A small paved arrival area between a Landmark Access Lane and a neutral Environmental Scenery building. It holds the Landmark checkpoint and may be identified by an Arrival Sign without making the building own or contain the associated Portfolio Record.
_Avoid_: Road checkpoint, doorstep marker

**Discovery**:
A portfolio story or credential tied to a Portfolio Record and directly readable in the Journey Atlas or presented during a Landmark Encounter.
_Avoid_: Unlock, reward

**Landmark Encounter**:
The moment in which a Visitor pauses at a Landmark checkpoint to hear Montasim's story and inspect its Discoveries. An Encounter never requires entering an Environmental Scenery building.
_Avoid_: Building interior, level transition

**Archive**:
A Journey Atlas collection associated with a Landmark that keeps every related Portfolio Record available without giving each item equal prominence during World Exploration.
_Avoid_: Hidden content, omitted content

**Featured Discovery**:
A selected project, achievement, or story given greater visibility in the Journey Atlas and its associated Landmark Encounter while remaining part of the same complete collection.
_Avoid_: Exclusive content

**Project Workshop**:
The named Projects collection in the Journey Atlas and its associated Landmark Encounter, containing curated Project Exhibits and the complete Project Archive. It is not an environmental building, and individual projects never become separate world Landmarks.
_Avoid_: Project campus, project building, one landmark per project

**Project Exhibit**:
A Project Workshop display that introduces a project, offers its available live, source, package, or release destinations outside the Portfolio World, and provides a contextual Briefing Entry. The Briefing distinguishes portfolio-description evidence from publicly inspectable artifacts.
_Avoid_: Embedded live demo

**Community Hall**:
The named Community collection in the Journey Atlas and its associated Landmark Encounter, owning the complete archive of Montasim's service, event-organizing, and student-organization contributions. It is not an environmental building; related Education Landmarks point to its records through Story Links instead of repeating them.
_Avoid_: Organization campus, community building, one landmark per club, duplicated education story

**Community Contribution**:
An organization or volunteering record presented in the Community Hall according to how Montasim contributed: leadership and service, event organizing, or student community participation.
_Avoid_: Social activity, extracurricular score

**Learning Journey**:
An evidence-based account of how Montasim learned, certified, practiced, applied, and produced results with a related group of technologies.
_Avoid_: Skill rating, proficiency bar, technology list

**Learning Room**:
A thematic Journey Atlas subdivision inside the Learning Library that groups related technologies and their evidence-backed Learning Journeys. A Learning Room is part of the Library Archive, not a physical room, and never becomes a separate world Landmark.
_Avoid_: Technology building, skill landmark, certificate room

**Story Link**:
A connection between related Discoveries in different Districts, such as community work associated with an education Landmark. It guides the Visitor to the canonical record instead of duplicating it.
_Avoid_: Duplicate discovery

**Story Card**:
A concise, warm-professional first-person narrative through which the Montasim Avatar explains a Discovery in the Journey Atlas or during a Landmark Encounter. It tells a story rather than repeating résumé language.
_Avoid_: Voice-over, article, data dump

**Journey Passport**:
A device-local record of Physical Visits and optional World Exploration completion. Reading a Journey Atlas entry does not create a stamp, and the Passport never awards points or restricts Portfolio Records.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, account, progression gate

**Suggested Destination**:
The next chronological Landmark recommended by the Guided Journey.
_Avoid_: Required objective, locked objective

**Active Destination**:
The Landmark currently identified by navigation guidance. The Visitor may accept the Suggested Destination or replace it with any Landmark selected from the Journey Map.
_Avoid_: Mandatory checkpoint

**Journey Map**:
The compact wayfinding overview used only during World Exploration, containing the Montasim Avatar, Landmarks, Physical Visit state, Suggested Destination, and Active Destination. It is distinct from the professional Journey Atlas and prioritizes travel over complete portfolio reading.
_Avoid_: Journey Atlas, geographic map, portfolio index

**Journey Line**:
The optional muted-terracotta line painted through the Portfolio World's roads that makes the Guided Journey and Active Destination visible without restricting World Exploration.
_Avoid_: Invisible wall, forced path

**Assisted Travel**:
An optional, Visitor-requested walk by the Montasim Avatar toward the Active Destination. It follows the precomputed sidewalk and building-access route, uses the designated left sidewalk as its primary travel lane, crosses only for an opposite-side destination approach, clears its destination guide on arrival, and can be interrupted by manual movement at any time.
_Avoid_: Teleport, forced movement
