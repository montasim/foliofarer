# Use stylized low-poly landmarks

The Portfolio World will interpret real schools, universities, workplaces, and other portfolio subjects as a cohesive low-poly town rather than reproducing their buildings exactly. Its “Monsoon Atlas” direction draws on a bright, rain-washed Bangladeshi daylight streetscape, with local identity carried through architecture, soft low-saturation color, vegetation, English-language signage, and restrained rickshaw-art geometry rather than literal replicas or Bengali text. The game will keep this single authored daylight setting rather than mirroring the Standard Portfolio's light and dark themes. Recognizable names, logos, and narrative details will preserve identity while the lighter asset scope supports fast loading, mobile play, and a consistent visual language; a muted-terracotta Journey Line will be the signature wayfinding element.
